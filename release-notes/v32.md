DQIX Enhanced v32: the European version, mods, HD text and a new Dynamic Widescreen.

**What's new since v31**

The European version
- **Dragon Quest IX (Europe)** builds and plays, in English, French,
  German, Italian or Spanish: **Game > Language** (the game starts in it
  next time; your adventure log is the same in every language). Thanks to
  romusan495.

Mods menu
- **Texture packs**: PNG pictures in the `textures` folder are drawn
  instead of the game's textures. To make one, turn on *Dump textures* and
  play: every texture shown lands in `textures\dump`. Redraw them, keep
  their names, put them in `textures`, then *Reload*. Hold **`** (left of
  1) to see the game's own, to compare. Thanks to romusan495, whose code
  the texture packs are built on.
- **Upscale the 3D textures** (off at first): xBR, 4x.
- **Music packs**: OGG, FLAC or WAV recordings in the `music` folder play
  instead of the game's songs, each named after the song it replaces
  (`BG_012.ogg`). *Open the music folder* lists every song in `songs.txt`,
  *Show each song's name as it starts* tells you which one is playing.
  Songs loop on the usual `LOOPSTART` / `LOOPLENGTH` tags; jingles (`ME_`)
  play once. The game's fades, pauses and stops carry over, and the
  original keeps its timing silently, so nothing waits differently.
- **Export the game's songs**: every song as a WAV file in `music\export`,
  with its loop points, so you can hear which name is which and line a
  recording up with its loop. An exported song can be edited and put back.
- How it all works: `docs/MODS.md`.

Widescreen
- **Basic Widescreen** and **Dynamic Widescreen (Experimental)** are their
  names now (View or Modern menu, F2).
- **Dynamic Widescreen, redone**: the field never moves. The map stays in
  the top left and the game's own windows over the field (the menu, its
  spell and item lists, the shop, the gold) move right, clear of it; the
  dialogue box stays in the middle with its name tag and Yes/No. Screens
  of their own (Stella's lists, Equipment, the quest and item lists,
  DQVC, making a companion) show at 4:3 on the right, with the map beside
  them. In battles the party's panels sit stacked in the bottom left,
  see-through with solid white lines, and while the attacks play they
  shrink to each name and HP/MP, with the battle messages moved right of
  them; the experience and level-up windows are see-through too.
- **Battle HUD for Basic Widescreen** (experimental, off at first: Modern >
  Widescreen: battle HUD): the party's panels stacked in the bottom left
  over the 3D. Thanks to romusan495.
- **The Alchemimicon** shows its recipe list big and the recipe's details
  small.
- **The game's movies** (the sacred tree) show both screens stacked, as on
  the DS.
- **Full-screen effects reach the sides**: the poison bog's flash, the
  "Some days later..." card, the Battle Records card, white flashes, and
  the sepia memory scenes (#18; thanks to celvrc).
- **Places made only for the DS's width** (the starry sky at the start, the
  way up to the Observatory) get black sides instead of showing past the
  edge of the scene. Found another one? Modern > "Widescreen: black sides
  in this place".
- **No pop-in or misplaced scenery at the sides**: very large things (the
  turnstile in Gortress's prison; thanks to celvrc) are drawn a good way
  before they come into view, and the railing by Coffinwell's well no
  longer flashes in the wrong place.

Graphics
- **HD text** (off at first: Graphics > HD text, or F1): the game's letters
  drawn again at full resolution with real fonts. Thanks to Arthur
  Ferreira Soares.
- **Character outlines** are an even, smooth line, only on models' edges.
  Graphics > Character outlines: Thin (the default), Medium, or Like the DS.
- **Soft shadows** stay a smooth gradient; Graphics > Soft shadows and
  glows turns them off for the DS's stepped look.
- **The inn's guests** (Quester's Rest) are drawn again: they were
  invisible in Enhanced mode.
- **No flicker of sleeping people** as the camera turns.

Installing
- **The installer fixes broken or old MSYS2 setups by itself** (`target
  not found`, `No module named 'yaml'`, "unable to lock database"), uses
  an MSYS2 you already have on any drive, and never deletes an old one.
  Thanks to everyone on the Discord who tracked this down.
- **A ROM inside a .zip works**: pick the zip, no unzipping needed.
- **Linux**: an AppImage that builds the game from your ROM, with a window
  to choose it and follow the build. Thanks to rakmahefa.
- When a build step fails without an error of its own, the build now says
  which step it was.

And more
- **Tab turns fast forward on and off** (or `fast_forward = hold` in
  settings.ini).
- **Game > Save file**: export your save, or import one (also DeSmuME's
  .dsv and melonDS saves). Your current save is backed up first.
- **Show on Discord what I'm playing** (Modern menu).
- **The controller's Share button** takes a screenshot.
- **Bug reports attach themselves**: after Send, the report is on your
  clipboard, so on the GitHub page it's just **Ctrl+V**, then **Create**.
- Fixed: Game > Controls (changing a key didn't finish in v31); leaving
  fullscreen gives the window back its borders; the menu bar in fullscreen
  shows over the picture and opens; quitting asks first; walking with the
  stick no longer goes the wrong way after a menu or a battle.

Still being looked into: the Zoom menu can smear in Dynamic Widescreen
(#16), and the light from the window in Upover's inn flickers (#19).

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`. **Already on v31?** The game
offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves
and settings are kept.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v32. European and USA players can't play together.

The plans for what comes next are in ROADMAP.md.
